# v1.5 My kitchen review — one settings hub, invites, confirmations, app name

Status: implemented on a feature branch for preview testing. **Not deployed; deploy approval is still required.** Responsive QA below is browser viewport simulation, not physical hardware.

## What changed
- **My kitchen is the single settings hub** (`kitchenModal()` in `src/frontend/personal-ui.ts`). Order: Household members & portions (inline names/portions, add/remove, save) → Family access → Menu preferences → Appearance → Currency & prices → Reminders & notifications → Data & backup → Account & security (collapsed). A section strip at the top jumps to each part (sticky on wide screens, one scrollable row on phones).
- Every settings entry opens it at the relevant section: sidebar ⌄, sidebar "Nhắc nhở & cài đặt" (reminders), Today "My kitchen", the generate-week dialog (menu), Connect family (family), the mobile **More** menu (new "My kitchen" entry) and `settingsModal()` callers in other modules. The old settings dialog in `app.ts` is now a thin delegate. Removed: "🏡 Mở Bếp của nhà mình (Gia đình & mã mời)" and the offline "Mở Tùy chọn & Cài đặt bếp" cross-link. It also opens offline: local sections keep working and Family access explains that invitations need internet.
- **Invitations consolidated**: one "Invite family" button for owners in Family access; active invitations with Revoke now live inside the invite sheet. "Join another family with a code" is one collapsed form; "Create a new family" is a secondary link to a create-only sheet (no second join form). Families list/switch appears when there is more than one. Today's "Invite family" button shows only for owners while the family has a single connected account (count cached per family from the members list). Device-only mode shows "Connect family" (sign in/register) plus a collapsed invitation-code form. The login invite field and `?invite=` link are unchanged.
- **Login flow**: `?join=1` (invite registration failed) no longer opens the full create/join page. A signed-in user without a selected family sees "Choose your family" (their own families) first; join (pre-filled with the failed code) and create are secondary. Members with a family never see create/join automatically.
- **Confirmations**: "Use device-only…" and "Sign out…" are buttons that open a reusable `confirmDialog()` (alertdialog, focus starts on Cancel, Tab trapped, Esc closes only the confirmation, buttons ≥44 px, bilingual). Text: "Rời không gian gia đình và dùng riêng trên máy này? Dữ liệu chỉ-trên-máy được giữ riêng, không gộp." plus a warning when a family sync is pending (`cloudConnection.dirty/conflict` or the `bep-cloud-pending` marker). Sign out calls the existing `signOutThisDevice()` — `household-ui.ts` was not edited.
- **English**: all strings moved into My kitchen use `tr(vi,en)`, including add/remove member dialogs, confirm buttons, placeholders, nav `aria-label`, sidebar summary. A Playwright check scans every text node and `aria-label/placeholder/title` in English My kitchen (all sections expanded) for Vietnamese diacritics, excluding `[data-user-content]` (family/member names).
- **Cross-tab bug**: the `storage` listener now compares against the scoped key (`storageScope + KEY`).
- **App name** (provisional, owner decision pending): manifest `name` "FamTray · Bếp Gia Đình", `short_name` "FamTray"; `application-name`/`apple-mobile-web-app-title` "FamTray" in `index.html` and the sign-in page; page titles "FamTray · Bếp Gia Đình"; the sign-in page now links the manifest, icon and apple-touch-icon and declares web-app capable. English UI brand is `APP_BRAND.en` (language.ts) = "FamTray"; Vietnamese UI keeps "Bếp Gia Đình". The sign-in page has its own `BRAND` constant (server module). Storage keys and internal ids are unchanged.

## Account menu (owner feedback, 1 Nov 2026)
Owner feedback from the iPhone preview: the big Today button “Bếp của nhà mình” and the round header avatar both opened My kitchen. Decision: keep the big button as the way into My kitchen; the avatar now opens a **short account menu**.
- **Avatar** (`updateAccountAvatar()` in `personal-ui.ts`, shell in `dist/index.html`): shows the first letter of the account display name (falls back to the username; the old hard-coded “N” is gone); device-only shows a neutral home icon. It is a 44×44 px button (`aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, bilingual `aria-label` “Tài khoản: <name>” / “Account: <name>”); the visible circle stays 32 px.
- **Menu content**: header with display name, @username, current family · role (signed in) or “Đang dùng trên máy này / Using this device only” (device-only). Signed in with more than one family: “Đổi gia đình / Switch family” list (current family checked; choosing it just closes the menu; others reuse the existing `switch` action with its unsynced-changes guard). Signed in: “Dùng riêng trên máy này… / Use device-only…” and “Đăng xuất… / Sign out…” reuse `confirmDeviceOnly()` / `confirmSignOut()`. Device-only: “Đăng nhập / Kết nối gia đình” reuses the existing connect flow (My kitchen → Family access). Signed in without a family: “Chọn gia đình…”. No My kitchen item and no language item (the language toggle is already in the header).
- **Behaviour**: focus moves to the first item; ↑/↓ (wrapping), Home/End; Esc, Tab, click outside or the avatar again close it; focus returns to the avatar (also after a confirmation dialog opened from the menu is cancelled). Rows ≥ 44 px (48 px min-height). Phones (< 600 px wide) get a bottom sheet with a backdrop and safe-area padding; wider screens a popover under the avatar clamped inside the viewport. Animation is a short fade/slide disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Dark mode has its own colours. The families list is fetched once per session when the menu opens online; offline it uses what is cached.
- **Other entry points** unchanged: Today “Bếp của nhà mình” button, sidebar ⌄ and “Nhắc nhở & cài đặt” (desktop/tablet), mobile More → My kitchen, and `settingsModal()` callers. On desktop Today the sidebar ⌄ and the big button are both visible (sidebar is persistent across screens, the big button is part of the page); left as the brief specifies.

## Limits / not done
- Recent activity log entries are stored Vietnamese text, so they are no longer shown in My kitchen (they were in the old settings dialog).
- The remove-member list (`manageMembers`, family-features.ts) and push/reminder sheets are other modules and keep their own wording.
- Backend notification text (`family.mts`, "Family Kitchen: test notification") and the service-worker push title were not renamed (other owners).
- Physical iPhone/iPad/Android hardware and installed-PWA naming were not tested. The account menu was checked by browser viewport simulation only (Chromium); VoiceOver/TalkBack were not run.
- Account-menu screenshots use mocked account/family responses (`kitchen-hub` harness), not the live service.
