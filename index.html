Conflicts and assumptions

  1. The March dimensioned upper plan p16 shows FFL 10.000, while p9, elevations and sections show RL 13.000. The model uses RL 13.000.
  2. March room uses are media / retreat / office; August B101 uses media / kitchen / bedroom suite. Both configurations are modelled, without presenting either as an approval determination.
  3. March external upper body is 10,560 mm, plus a separate 100 mm edge offset. August B101 labels 10,660 mm overall. Its bedroom-width and finished-wall chains do not consistently close. August right-room width is fitted to the outer shell; confirm with the designer.
  4. Ground overall depth differs: March 7,595 mm, August 7,615 mm. The two models use their respective values.
  5. August window labels use brick-course codes (e.g. 8x2170), while March supplies millimetre heights. Several August widths differ (e.g. W09 2,890 vs March 3,100). No definitive course-height conversion is given; the model retains March mm heights and flags replacement widths.
  6. Studio exterior registration, driveway and site boundary shape are traced for context, with approximate terrain. The DRA driveway approval is included as reference; its crossover profiles are not reproduced as an engineering road model.
  7. The existing house has a context layout traced from sheet 6, floor RL 9.600. Complete room dimensions, window schedules and roof details are absent for the house; it is not a fully measured reconstruction.
  8. The exterior palette in March shows Royal Quarter / Double Bass; the design presentation later specifies White Exchange Half / Pewter Frame / timber. Colour appearance is an approximate screen interpretation.
  9. Furniture, appliances, cabinets, doors, first stair winder, guard details, shower fittings, fixture locations and finish textures are illustrative. The model is not photogrammetry or a photorealistic photographic rendering.
  10. Walk-through gives a 1.58 m eye height above the studio floor and free movement; collision and accessibility compliance are not simulated. Point measurement is on a chosen floor plane, not snap-to-building geometry.
  11. GLB files preserve the same geometry and material colours for Blender / other glTF viewers. Procedural finish textures and browser daylight shader are viewer-only; exports carry PBR colours without those textures.
